Loans & Credit

24-hour promise · Transparent rates · Tailored repayment.

Boda bodas, market vendors, traders, SMEs and solidarity groups — we design our credit products around how you actually earn.

How the Loans service works

  1. Consult: Call, WhatsApp or visit Hanora Plaza, Zana. We explain available products on the same day.
  2. Apply: Complete a simple application. State the purpose, amount and preferred repayment frequency.
  3. KYC & Visit: Provide National ID, a passport photo and a local referee or stage leader. We conduct a respectful workplace visit.
  4. Approve & Disburse: Decision within 24 hours. Funds via Mobile Money (MTN / Airtel), bank or cash.
  5. Repay & Grow: Small daily or weekly repayments. Consistent repayment unlocks higher limits.

Who this is for

  • Boda boda cyclists & taxi stages
  • Market vendors & food sellers
  • Retail shops, kiosks & salons
  • Trader associations & savings groups
  • SMEs seeking working capital

Typical turnaround

24 hrs

From complete application to approval decision. Disbursement immediately after signing.

Respectful tenancy · Prompt remittance · Verified contractors.

What we do as your property managers

  1. Onboard: We meet you, inspect your property and agree a management mandate aligned with the Landlord and Tenant Act.
  2. Source & Vet: We advertise, shortlist and vet tenants — National ID, references and income viability verified.
  3. Collect & Remit: Rent is collected on the due date with official e-receipts. Net amounts remitted to your account punctually.
  4. Maintain: Routine and emergency repairs coordinated via vetted contractors. You approve major spending in advance.
  5. Report: Transparent monthly statement with photo condition reports, utility reconciliations and arrears status.

Properties we manage

  • Apartment blocks & units
  • Single rental homes & duplexes
  • Shops & commercial suites
  • Office floors & co-working
  • Student & staff hostels

Reporting frequency

Monthly

Detailed statements plus quarterly photographic condition inspections. Diaspora clients get priority WhatsApp updates.

Real Estate

Verified listings · Title due diligence · Fraud-safe process.

From residential plots in Kira to commercial suites in Nakasero and lakefront land in Entebbe — we protect every step of your transaction.

Land & Plot Sales
Property Sales & Purchase
Leasing & Rental Listings
Title Search & Registry Checks

Conveyance & Legal Support

A safe real estate journey

  1. Consult: Tell us your budget, area and tenure preference (Mailo, Freehold, Leasehold). We shortlist verified options.
  2. View: Site visits on your schedule. We confirm physical boundaries with a trusted surveyor.
  3. Due Diligence: Land registry search, encumbrance check, family consent and boundary verification before any commitment.
  4. Agree & Transfer: Negotiated offer, sale agreement and title transfer handled alongside a trusted advocate.
  5. Close: Keys, title or occupancy letter delivered in person. Post-handover support included.

Tenures we handle

  • Mailo land titles
  • Freehold titles
  • Leasehold (government & private)
  • Block & plot (surveyed land)
  • Strata-titled apartments

Fraud protection built in

100%

We never recommend purchase without a full registry search, family consent and boundary survey.

The Loan Journey

Clear, fast steps from application to disbursement.

Designed for market vendors, boda boda cyclists, retailers, and small businesses. Enjoy respectful service, transparent terms, and our 24-hour decision promise.

1

Enquiry & Consultation

Contact us via phone or WhatsApp (+256 787 165 366), or visit our branch at Hanora Plaza, Zana. We explain available loan options clearly on the same day.

Same-day response
2

Simple Application

State your loan purpose, requested amount, and preferred daily or weekly repayment plan using a simple, straightforward application form in English or Luganda.

No complex paperwork
3

KYC & Workplace Visit

Provide a copy of your National ID, passport photo, and a local referee or stage/group leader reference. Our loan officer conducts a quick, respectful workplace visit.

Verified & respectful
4

24-Hour Approval Decision

Our credit team reviews your actual business cash flow and provides an approval decision within 24 hours with transparent terms and legal UMRA rate compliance.

24-Hour promise

6
