The Loan Journey

Clear, fast steps from application to disbursement.

Designed for market vendors, boda boda cyclists, retailers, and small businesses. Enjoy respectful service, transparent terms, and our 24-hour decision promise.

1

Enquiry & Consultation

Contact us via phone or WhatsApp (+256 787 165 366), or visit our branch at Hanora Plaza, Zana. We explain available loan options clearly on the same day.

Same-day response
2

Simple Application

State your loan purpose, requested amount, and preferred daily or weekly repayment plan using a simple, straightforward application form in English or Luganda.

No complex paperwork
3

KYC & Workplace Visit

Provide a copy of your National ID, passport photo, and a local referee or stage/group leader reference. Our loan officer conducts a quick, respectful workplace visit.

Verified & respectful
4

24-Hour Approval Decision

Our credit team reviews your actual business cash flow and provides an approval decision within 24 hours with transparent terms and legal UMRA rate compliance.

24-Hour promise
5

Immediate Disbursement

Sign your clear loan agreement showing total costs and repayment dates. Funds are released promptly via Mobile Money (MTN / Airtel), bank transfer, or cash.

Mobile Money or Bank
6

Flexible Repayments & Growth

Make small daily or weekly repayments matching your earning cycle with instant receipts. Punctual borrowers earn higher loan limits and rapid loan renewals.

Daily & weekly options
UMRA Regulated Transparent pricing compliant with Ugandan Tier 4 microfinance regulations.
24-Hour Decision Quick credit access without the long waiting times and heavy demands of commercial banks.
Tailored Repayment Daily collection options for market vendors and weekly schedules for retail shops.
Loyalty Growth Consistent repayment records unlock higher amounts and dedicated business credit.
